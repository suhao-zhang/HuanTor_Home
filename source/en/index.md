<div class="section-head section-head--left">
  <h1>We put large models on the production line</h1>
  <p>Registered in Huixian, Xinxiang, Henan. One team takes a project from requirements to operations.</p>
</div>

<div class="about__text">
  <p>Xinxiang Huantuo Technology Co., Ltd. does one thing: it turns a company's own data and business processes into systems that actually run. Documents, process parameters, after-sales tickets, operating figures — the question is always how these become a working question-answering system, a detection model, or a platform people use every day.</p>
  <p>The rest follows from that. How those systems get onto the cloud, how they stay stable, and how they keep improving after go-live. We treat this as one delivery chain rather than handing it between three different vendors.</p>
</div>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 72px;">
  <h2>How a project runs</h2>
  <p>Four stages, each with something you can hold and sign off</p>
</div>

<div class="ht-timeline ht-stagger">
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Requirements diagnosis</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">First, whether it should be built at all</span></div>
    <p>We ask who uses the feature, how the process runs today, where the data lives, and what number decides success. Some requirements get rejected by us at this stage.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Output:</b> scenario list, data readiness assessment, feasibility and risk notes</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Solution design</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">Choices and boundaries</span></div>
    <p>Architecture sketch, model and retrieval approach, integration points, effort breakdown — and an explicit line between phase one and everything after it.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Output:</b> technical proposal, milestone plan, itemised quotation</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Build and deploy</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">Running in your environment</span></div>
    <p>Integration testing per milestone. Model quality is checked against an evaluation set agreed in advance, not by impression. Permissions, logging and backups are configured during deployment, not after an incident.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Output:</b> working system, test and verification records, deployment and operations documentation</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Operations and iteration</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">Data flows back</span></div>
    <p>Go-live is not the end. We review real usage and failure cases, then adjust retrieval, refill the knowledge base, and tune prompts and process until the result stays above the usable line.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Output:</b> usage dashboard, improvement backlog, version plan</p>
  </div>
</div>
