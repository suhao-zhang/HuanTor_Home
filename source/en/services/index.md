<div class="section-head section-head--left">
  <h1>Three product lines, six capabilities</h1>
  <p>Composed to fit the scenario. Every stage has a named deliverable.</p>
</div>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 8px;">
  <h2>Product lines</h2>
  <p>Where a first engagement usually starts</p>
</div>

<div class="ht-modes ht-stagger">
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Enterprise knowledge base and Q&amp;A</h3>
    <p>Turns policies, process documents and after-sales records into a question-answering system and a ticket assistant.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Document parsing, chunking and retrieval chain</li>
      <li>Evaluation set agreed before build starts</li>
      <li>Deployed in your environment; data stays inside</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Industrial vision and equipment data</h3>
    <p>Defect detection, parameter optimisation and line-side dashboards with the data loop closed.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Judgement logic designed per process step</li>
      <li>Miss and false-positive rates measured on real lines</li>
      <li>Camera and PLC integration handled by us</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>SaaS systems and GEO</h3>
    <p>Productise a business system, then run content operations and AI-search visibility alongside it.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Multi-tenant architecture and permissions</li>
      <li>Management dashboards for operating figures</li>
      <li>Visibility measured by citations in AI answers</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 72px;">
  <h2>What you receive at each stage</h2>
  <p>The right-hand column is what we sign off against</p>
</div>

  <thead>
    <tr><th>Stage</th><th>Deliverable</th><th>Acceptance</th></tr>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 72px;">
  <h2>Engagement models</h2>
  <p>Chosen by the nature of the work, not one contract template</p>
</div>

<div class="ht-modes ht-stagger">
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Project delivery</h3>
    <p>A defined scope with defined milestones.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Proposal plus itemised quotation</li>
      <li>Source code, documentation and environment handed over</li>
      <li>Defect fixes covered during the warranty period</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Ongoing development</h3>
    <p>For products that keep evolving without an in-house team.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Quarterly or annual engineering and operations capacity</li>
      <li>Fixed cadence for planning and review</li>
      <li>Monitoring, backups and incident response agreed in writing</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Joint operations</h3>
    <p>For SaaS and GEO, which are continuous by nature.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>We provide technology and operations; you provide scenario and content</li>
      <li>Judged on measurable indicators</li>
      <li>Commercial terms discussed case by case</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<p style="margin-top: 48px; color: var(--muted);">Data handling: private deployment is the default. Where a third-party model API is genuinely needed, we state in advance which data leaves your network, where it goes, and how long it is retained — and we do not use your documents or business data for model training or any secondary purpose.</p>
