  <h1>Four roles, always worth a conversation</h1>
  <p>Location: 4F, Jincheng Department Store, Huixian, Xinxiang, Henan, China</p>
</div>

<div class="about__text">
  <p>We do not hire by headcount targets. What a project needs is people who can carry a stretch of the chain on their own: ask a client what they actually need, get a model to the agreed quality line, deploy a system into somebody else's machine room, and then keep reading the data after go-live.</p>
  <p>Each role below lists what we expect you to bring and what we are willing to teach. Do not screen yourself out using the second list.</p>
</div>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 72px;">
  <h2>From résumé to offer</h2>
  <p>Four steps, at most two of them face to face</p>
</div>

<div class="ht-timeline ht-stagger">
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Show what you have made</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">résumé or artefacts</span></div>
    <p>No cover letter needed. Send one thing we can open: a repository, something you deployed, technical notes you wrote, even a set of materials you organised. Education and years of experience are welcome in the message, but they are not the first filter.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>You prepare:</b> a short introduction plus one thing that can be looked at</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>One conversation</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">about an hour</span></div>
    <p>We talk about one specific thing you did: how you judged it, where it went wrong, what you would do differently. We also open up a real project we are on, so you can ask anything — including pay and working rhythm.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Decided here:</b> which of the four roles the direction lands in</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>A small task</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">take it home, no time limit</span></div>
    <p>A slice cut from a real project: propose a chunking plan for a batch of documents, wire one page to an API, or write an incident review. The point is how you think and how you hand something over — the task content and your conclusions are not used for commercial delivery.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>We look at:</b> whether the trade-offs are explainable, whether the output is directly usable</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-step">
    <div class="ht-step__head"><h3>Role and terms</h3><span class="ht-step__tag">in person</span></div>
    <p>We go through the task together, then settle the specifics: which project, who you work with, terms and probation. We will not ask you to come in and work before this step.</p>
    <p class="ht-step__out"><b>Output:</b> a verbal conclusion plus something in writing</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section-head" style="margin-top: 72px;">
  <h2>How it is here</h2>
  <p>Said up front, so nobody is disappointed after joining</p>
</div>

<div class="ht-modes ht-stagger">
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>Builders decide</h3>
    <p>Solutions are written by the people who will maintain them, not by people who never touch the code.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Scoping, design and delivery usually the same people</li>
      <li>No reporting material produced for its own sake</li>
      <li>Technical trade-offs are argued out with reasons</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>You touch real things</h3>
    <p>Client sites, live data, production equipment — not practice projects.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>You see how a requirement changes three times</li>
      <li>Model quality has an evaluation set; good or bad is a number</li>
      <li>Systems run in someone else's machine room, so incidents are real</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="ht-mode">
    <h3>What we will not promise</h3>
    <p>Better to read it here than to be disappointed later.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>No guarantee of no overtime: releases have crunch</li>
      <li>No guarantee of fully remote work: roles are based in Huixian, exceptions discussed</li>
      <li>This page states no salary range or headcount — terms are set in the conversation and the contract</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
