## What the company does

An AI application and software delivery company. It builds systems on top of an enterprise's own
data and business processes, and keeps them running after go-live. Six directions:

1. Large-model applications — fine-tuning and inference deployment, RAG knowledge bases,
   agent workflows, integrated into existing business systems.
2. Industrial AI — visual defect detection, equipment data analysis, line-side agents and alerting,
   with judgement logic designed per process step.
3. Software development — admin consoles, mini-programs and custom business systems,
   designed around the real process and permission boundaries.
4. Cloud services — cloud architecture, containerised deployment, monitoring and managed operations.
5. SaaS products and operations — multi-tenant platforms, content operations, management dashboards.
6. GEO (generative engine optimisation) — making a brand retrievable, citable and recommendable
   inside AI search and large-model answers.

## How a project is delivered

Four stages, each with a named deliverable and an acceptance definition:
requirements diagnosis → solution design → build and deploy → operations and iteration.
Model quality is judged against an evaluation set agreed before the build, not by impression.
Private deployment is the default; data stays inside the client's network. Where a third-party
model API is required, what leaves the network, where it goes and how long it is retained is
stated in advance. Client documents and business data are never used for model training.
